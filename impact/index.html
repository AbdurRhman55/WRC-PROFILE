How We Ensure Quality

Monitoring & Evaluation Framework

A rigorous, multi-layered M&E system that tracks every intervention from delivery to long-term outcome.

Step 01

Third-Party Audits

Independent auditors verify financial integrity, program delivery, and beneficiary outcomes against internationally recognized standards.

Step 02

Real-Time Data Tracking

Digital dashboards capture field data as it happens, enabling rapid response to emerging needs and evidence-based decision making.

Step 03

Quality Standards (ISO Compliance)

Programs are aligned with ISO 9001 and ISO 22000 quality management systems to ensure consistent, reliable delivery across all sectors.

Step 04

Community Feedback Loops

Beneficiary satisfaction surveys, community scorecards, and grievance mechanisms ensure programs remain responsive and community-driven.

Step 05

Annual Impact Assessments

Comprehensive annual evaluations synthesize data across sectors to measure long-term outcomes and inform strategic planning.

Case Studies

In-depth look at how WRC programs transform lives, backed by data and beneficiary voices.

WASH

Clean Water for Nowshera: A Community Transformed

In 2023, WRC installed 35 deep tube wells across 12 villages in Nowshera District, providing clean drinking water to over 8,000 residents previously dependent on contaminated sources.

8,200
People Served
35
Wells Built
12
Villages

"Before the wells, my children walked 3 km daily for water that made them sick. Now they have clean water at school and more time to study."

— Fatima Bibi, Mother of 4, Nowshera

Outcome: Waterborne diseases reduced by 62% in target villages.

Education

Bright Futures: Orphan Education in Peshawar

WRC's orphan sponsorship program enrolled 1,200 children in Peshawar, providing school kits, uniforms, tuition support, and after-school tutoring for 18 months.

Food Security

Emergency Flood Relief in Sindh: Feeding Thousands

During the 2023 Pakistan floods, WRC distributed 12,000 emergency food packages and set up 8 community kitchens in Sukkhur and Dadu districts within 72 hours.

48,000

"When the flood destroyed everything, WRC arrived with food for our entire village within three days. They saved us from starvation."

— Muhammad Raza, Farmer, Sukkur

Outcome: Zero malnutrition deaths in target areas; 100% distribution transparency.
