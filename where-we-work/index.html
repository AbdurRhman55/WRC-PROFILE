Province Spotlight

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

From the mountain valleys of Swat to urban Peshawar, WRC delivers emergency flood relief, healthcare, and education programs across KP's most vulnerable communities.

Mountain Flood Relief

Emergency shelter, food, and medical aid for flood-hit communities in Swat, Dir, and Chitral valleys.

Healthcare Access

Free medical camps, mobile clinics, and maternal health services in remote mountain villages.

Education Initiatives

School rebuilding, orphan sponsorship, and education kits for children displaced by conflict and disasters.

Punjab

WRC's South Punjab operations focus on WASH infrastructure, emergency food distribution, and nutrition support for rural farming communities.

WASH Infrastructure

Bore wells, hand pumps, and sanitation facilities for underserved rural villages in South Punjab.

Food Aid

Emergency ration packs and seasonal food distribution for flood-affected and food-insecure families.

Nutrition Programs

Therapeutic feeding centers and maternal nutrition education to combat malnutrition in children under 5.

Sindh

In Sindh's desert belt, WRC combats drought and heat with mobile health units, water trucking, and community resilience programs across Tharparkar and surrounding districts.

Mobile Health Units

Equipped vans providing free consultations, medicines, and maternal care in remote desert settlements.

Drought Relief

Emergency water trucking, livestock support, and drought-resistant crop distribution for farming families.

Community Resilience

Disaster preparedness training, early warning systems, and community-led emergency response teams.

Province Spotlight

Balochistan

In Pakistan's most underserved province, WRC provides clean water through hand-dug wells and solar-powered water systems, bringing reliable water access to remote desert communities.

Clean Water Wells

Hand-dug and mechanized wells providing year-round clean drinking water to villages with no prior water infrastructure.

Solar Energy Solutions

Solar-powered water pumps and community lighting systems for off-grid settlements in remote areas.

Community Health

Basic health units, maternal care, and nutrition support for nomadic and semi-nomadic populations.
