Our process

How we work

From the first funding lead to lasting change on the ground — a disciplined, transparent path that every project moves through, start to finish.

01

Funding opportunity

We scan donor calls, government grants, and partnership openings, screening each against community needs and our technical capacity before pursuing it.

02

Proposal development

Technical and finance teams co-author a proposal with clear objectives, a realistic budget, and evidence drawn from prior assessments in the field.

03

Project award

Once selected, we formalize the agreement with the donor, confirm compliance requirements, and mobilize the core project team.

04

Planning

We map scope, timelines, and responsibilities with local stakeholders, turning the proposal into a workable operational plan.

05

Implementation

Activities move onto the ground, delivered with technical rigor and close coordination with the communities and institutions we serve.

06

Monitoring & evaluation

Indicators are tracked against the plan, so we can catch issues early and adjust course before they affect outcomes.

07

Reporting

Results, spending, and lessons learned are documented clearly for funders, partners, and the communities involved.

08

Project closure

Deliverables are finalized, accounts reconciled, and the project formally handed over to the communities and institutions that will sustain it.

Where it all leads

Community impact

Every stage exists to serve this outcome — measurable, lasting improvement in the lives of the people we work with.
