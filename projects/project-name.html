Emergency Flood Relief 2025 - Swat Valley

Providing immediate life-saving assistance to flood-affected communities across Swat Valley, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.

Duration Jan 2025 - Dec 2025
Beneficiaries 15,000
Budget PKR 45M
Donors 3
Project Overview

About This Project

The Emergency Flood Relief 2025 project was launched in response to the devastating monsoon floods that struck Swat Valley in early January 2025. The catastrophic flooding destroyed homes, washed away critical infrastructure, and displaced thousands of families from their villages.

WRC deployed rapid response teams within 48 hours of the disaster, providing emergency shelter, food supplies, clean drinking water, and essential medical care to the most severely affected communities. The project has since expanded to include temporary housing reconstruction, livelihood restoration, and psychosocial support services.

Working closely with local government authorities and community leaders, WRC ensures that aid reaches the most vulnerable populations, including women, children, elderly persons, and persons with disabilities who face heightened risks during humanitarian crises.

Project Objectives

1

Provide Emergency Shelter & Food

Deliver emergency relief kits including tents, blankets, food rations, and clean drinking water to 5,000 displaced families within the first 30 days of deployment.

2

Restore Access to Healthcare

Establish mobile health units and temporary medical camps to provide primary healthcare, maternal services, and disease prevention in flood-affected areas.

3

Rebuild Livelihoods & Infrastructure

Support the reconstruction of damaged homes, schools, and water systems while providing livelihood grants to help families resume their economic activities.

4

Strengthen Community Resilience

Train local volunteers and community-based organizations in disaster preparedness, early warning systems, and emergency response protocols for future flood events.

Target Groups

Flood-affected Families Women & Children Elderly Persons Disabled Persons

Project Timeline

January 2025

Rapid Needs Assessment

WRC rapid response teams conducted comprehensive needs assessments across 12 affected villages in Swat Valley, identifying 5,000 displaced families requiring immediate assistance.

February 2025

Planning & Resource Mobilization

Developed detailed implementation plan, secured donor funding, pre-positioned relief supplies, and coordinated with provincial disaster management authorities.

March - November 2025

Implementation Phase

Full-scale relief distribution, mobile health operations, shelter reconstruction, livelihood grants, and community resilience training across all target villages.

December 2025

Monitoring & Evaluation

Final evaluation, impact assessment, beneficiary feedback collection, and lessons learned documentation for future emergency responses.
