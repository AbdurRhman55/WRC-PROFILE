Careers

Join Our Team

We're looking for passionate professionals committed to humanitarian service. Build a meaningful career while making an impact on millions of lives.

Role Description

The Field Officer will coordinate directly with beneficiary communities in Peshawar district, overseeing program delivery, data collection, and field reporting for WRC's health and education programs.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct field visits and community assessments
  • Coordinate with local leaders and stakeholders
  • Collect and submit beneficiary data and reports
  • Ensure compliance with WRC field protocols

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Social Sciences or related field
  • 2+ years of NGO field experience
  • Fluent in Pashto and Urdu; English preferred
  • Valid motorcycle driving license
Deadline: 15 Sep 2026

Role Description

The Project Manager will lead multi-sector humanitarian projects across KP and Punjab provinces, managing teams, donor reporting, and strategic partnerships to deliver measurable impact.

Key Responsibilities

  • End-to-end project lifecycle management
  • Budget planning, monitoring, and financial reporting
  • Stakeholder coordination with donors and government
  • Team leadership and capacity building

Requirements

  • Master's degree in Development Studies or MBA
  • 5+ years in project management (humanitarian sector)
  • PMP or PRINCE2 certification preferred
  • Strong donor reporting and proposal writing skills
Deadline: 20 Sep 2026

Role Description

The M&E Specialist will design and implement robust monitoring and evaluation frameworks for WRC programs, ensuring data-driven decision-making and accountability to donors and beneficiaries.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop M&E frameworks and logframes
  • Train field staff on data collection tools
  • Conduct baseline, midline, and endline surveys
  • Produce monthly and quarterly M&E reports

Requirements

  • Master's degree in Statistics, Economics, or Social Sciences
  • 3+ years in M&E for humanitarian programs
  • Proficiency in SPSS, Stata, or KoboToolbox
  • Strong analytical and report-writing skills
Deadline: 25 Sep 2026

Internships

Launch Your Humanitarian Career

Our internship program gives university students and recent graduates hands-on experience in humanitarian operations, development research, and nonprofit management across Pakistan.

University Partnerships

WRC partners with leading universities including University of Peshawar, Islamia College University, and Pakistan Institute of Development Economics (PIDE) to offer structured internship placements.

Duration

Internships range from 3 to 6 months, with flexible scheduling to accommodate academic calendars. Extensions are available for exceptional performers.
